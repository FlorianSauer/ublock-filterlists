! Title: ublock-filterlists – heise.de
! Description: Hide heise+ paywall teasers, self-promo, affiliate best lists, and unwanted modules on heise.de
! Homepage: https://github.com/FlorianSauer/ublock-filterlists
! Expires: 2 days
! Version: 20261116160000
! Last modified: 2026-11-16
! Licence: MIT
!
! Anchor policy: data-* attributes, href paths, ARIA roles, asset file
! names. Text anchors only where the site offers no structural hook
! (heise.de is German-only, its editorial labels are not localized).

! ---------- heise+ / paywall teasers ----------
! Badge asset file name, covers light and negative variants:
heise.de##article:has(img[src*="heise_plus"])

! ---------- Self-promo / marketing modules ----------
! Every marketing module carries the "Eigenwerbung" label, the
! attribute alone is enough and survives label renames:
heise.de##div[data-component="MarketingTeaserModule"]
! Event / webinar / workshop promos (promo chip inside the teaser):
heise.de##a[data-component="TeaserLinkContainer"]:has(span:has-text(heise-Angebot))
heise.de##div[data-component="NewsletterModule"]
heise.de##div[data-component="PreisvergleichModule"]
heise.de##div[data-module-name="HeiseJobsModule"]
heise.de##div[data-component="TeasersModule"]:has(article[data-component="TeaserContainer"] a[href*="heise-academy.de"])

! ---------- Series teasers ----------
! Series name in the kicker component (a brand name, never localized):
heise.de##article[data-component="TeaserContainer"]:has([data-component="TeaserKicker"]:has-text(TGIQF))
heise.de##article[data-component="TeaserContainer"]:has([data-component="TeaserKicker"]:has-text(heiseshow))
heise.de##article[data-component="TeaserContainer"]:has([data-component="TeaserKicker"]:has-text(3003))

! ---------- External content ----------
! Consent boxes ("Empfohlener redaktioneller Inhalt / Mit Ihrer
! Zustimmung…"): the opt-in widget carries an accept button. Provider
! agnostic (measured: Podigee, "Preisvergleichinternetservices") and
! robust against the data-component attribute, which varies per
! template (news pages omit it). Video tiles stay: they are
! figure/img without any button:
heise.de##a-opt-in:has(button)
! Podigee leftovers without a consent form (stealth player footer):
heise.de##a-opt-in[type*="Podigee"]

! ---------- Podcast bars ----------
! Four self-promo podcast rows on the front page:
heise.de##div[data-component="PodcastModule"]

! ---------- heise+ / subscription promos ----------
! Nav button "heise+ entdecken" and Abo link (affiliate):
heise.de##a[href*="/plus/abo"]
heise.de##a[href="/plus"]
! Sticky action bar at the bottom ("1 Jahr mit 50 % Rabatt"), floats at the
! bottom edge (a-sticky-footer, 1104x106, z-index 5999996; 2026-11-15, 1).
! Anchored on the tag alone so variants without data-component still match:
heise.de##a-sticky-footer
! "beliebt bei Abonnenten" (plus-only content; now empty because the
! plus teasers are hidden): the box itself, and on the front page the
! whole column in the 3-column group (flex: 1 keeps its 336px even when
! the box is empty; measured 1 of 3 columns, 2026-11-16). Source text is
! lowercase "beliebt" — the anchor is a case-insensitive regex, the
! plain string form misses (measured 0 vs. 1):
heise.de##a-collapse[data-component="Box"]:has(header:has-text(/beliebt\s+bei\s+abonnenten/i))
heise.de##a-collapse-group > div:has(header:has-text(/beliebt\s+bei\s+abonnenten/i))

! ---------- Best lists (affiliate articles) ----------
heise.de##article:has(a[href*="/bestenlisten/"])
heise.de##div[data-component="TeasersModule"]:has(a[href*="/bestenlisten/"])
! Optional: the tab in the topic strip
!heise.de##li:has(a[href*="/bestenlisten/"])

! ---------- Other unwanted modules ----------
! Video module ("Aktuelle Videos"):
heise.de##div[data-component="TeasersModule"]:has([data-component="ModuleHeader"]:has-text(Aktuelle Videos))
! English news module (identified by data-content-id, no text anchor):
heise.de##div[data-module-name="TeasersModule"][data-content-id="4624608"]
! Telepolis:
heise.de##div[data-component="TeasersModule"]:has([data-component="ModuleHeader"]:has-text(Telepolis))
! c't NerdWord podcast bar (BackgroundContainer):
heise.de##div[data-component="BackgroundContainer"]:has-text(NerdWord)
! c't NerdWord puzzle module ("Welches nerdige Lösungswort suchen wir
! heute? Sie haben sechs Versuche.", HeroModule with h2 heading). The
! podcast rule above misses it (measured: 0 puzzle texts inside
! BackgroundContainer); 2026-11-16, 1:
heise.de##div[data-module-name="HeroModule"]:has(h2:has-text(/nerdword/i))

! ---------- Article-page extras ----------
! "Lesen Sie auch" recommendation box (measured on an article page,
! the box carries its own data-component):
heise.de##div[data-component="RecommendationBox"]
! Newsletter signup box in the article sidebar. This variant carries
! no data-* (which is why the NewsletterModule rule misses it);
! anchored on the form action path two levels down:
heise.de##aside:has(> div > form[action*="/newsletter/"])
! Newsletter promo banner ("markenbanner" placements, image link to
! /newsletter/anmeldung.html?id=…). Hides the wrapper block, not just
! the link (footer links to the newsletter page stay untouched);
! measured structure: div > figure > a:
heise.de##div:has(> figure > a[href*="/newsletter/anmeldung"])
! Branding banner family: image link wrapped in figure.branding, e.g.
! the pro.heise.de/security/ campaign box at the article end
! (2026-11-16, 1). Anchored on the wrapper class because the campaign
! URL (LPID=…) changes per campaign while the structure stays:
heise.de##div:has(> figure.branding)
! Translation notice ("This article is also available in English"):
! details notice with a semantic dismiss action; matches both
! language directions (hide-en-pointer / hide-de-pointer):
heise.de##details:has(button[data-action*="pointer"])

! ---------- Advertising ----------
! Classic ad modules (measured on a topic page: 4 NewAdModule +
! 5 AdModule instances, grey placeholders inside):
heise.de##[data-component="NewAdModule"]
heise.de##[data-module-name="AdModule"]
! Ad tiles scattered through teaser grids ("graue Dosen"): the empty
! placeholder surface. Anchored on the placeholder class pair (light +
! dark mode variant of the same design token — not a hash, it changes
! only with the design system); the wrapper collapses to 0 height:
heise.de##div[class*="bg-heiseblau-100"][class*="bg-gray-900"]
! Empty grey ad band at page end (ad-desktop-group-2 shell, 1104x294;
!   2026-11-15, 1) and the bare slot shells (ad-desktop/ad-mobile-group,
!   2026-11-15, 3):
heise.de##div[class*="a-layout--bg-grey"]:has([class*="ad-desktop-group"], [class*="ad-mobile-group"])
heise.de##div[class*="ad-desktop-group"], div[class*="ad-mobile-group"]
! Sidebar "Anzeige" placeholder box (grey img-ad shell, 336x283; 2026-11-15, 1)
heise.de##aside[class*="img-ad"]

! Title: ublock-filterlists – tumblr.com
! Description: Hide the cookie banner (CMP), login nags/dialogs, and the scroll lock on tumblr.com
! Homepage: https://github.com/FlorianSauer/ublock-filterlists
! Expires: 2 days
! Version: 20261115140000
! Last modified: 2026-11-15
! Licence: MIT
!
! Anchor policy: ARIA roles, href paths, DOM structure. No text anchors
! (the site is multi-lingual), no generated class names.

! ---------- Cookie banner ----------
! Tumblr's CMP full-screen overlay (vendor library class, not a build hash):
tumblr.com##.cmp-components-modal__screen-overlay
! Harder, survives CMP class renames (the path prefix outlives
! cmp-23 -> cmp-24); uncomment if the class-based rule ever breaks:
!tumblr.com##||assets.tumblr.com/pop/vendor/cmp-$script

! ---------- Scroll login dialog ----------
! aria-modal + role + auth links (language independent):
tumblr.com##div[role="dialog"][aria-modal="true"]:has(a[href^="/auth/"])

! ---------- Pitch component ----------
! Hits both variants: the fixed bottom nag bar and the right sign-up box.
! Anchored on the bolded pitch fragment (strong) plus a button with
! aria-label; :upward(1) takes the colored frame with it (otherwise an
! empty colored strip stays visible). Measured: exactly 2 hits,
! 0 false positives on a permalink login-wall page.
tumblr.com##div:has(> div > strong):has(> div > button[aria-label]):upward(1)

! ---------- Scroll lock ----------
! The dialog injects <style>body{overflow:hidden;padding-right:8px}</style>
! into its own tree, which keeps applying while the dialog is hidden:
tumblr.com##body:style(overflow: auto !important; padding-right: 0 !important)
